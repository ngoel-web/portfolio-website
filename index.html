Executive Overview

Bridging Technical Rigor with Global Strategic Delivery

Over two decades of multidisciplinary engineering stewardship across high-speed rail, international airport expansions, and sustainable urban townships.

Leading the strategic production growth at Pinnacle Future Build, Naresh Goel champions digital transformation across the AECO industry—moving beyond isolated tools to connected, interoperable workflows that deliver certainty from design to handover.

With 24+ years of extensive managerial experience—including 8 transformative years in the Gulf managing complex joint ventures and multinational engineering consortiums—his background spans the full lifecycle of infrastructure delivery. His core portfolio covers responsibilities across MEP engineering, high-capacity Tunnel Ventilation Systems (TVS), building services, and enterprise-scale BIM implementation.
